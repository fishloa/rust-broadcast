## What this settles

1. `@par` **is** `(@width × sarx) : (@height × sary)` — the arithmetic `transmux::dash` uses
   (`par_of`), and it is a *shall* when `@par`, `@width`, `@height` are all present. (Not stated: that
   the written value must be gcd-reduced; both `"16:9"` examples are reduced. We write it reduced.)
2. `@width`/`@height` are counts of samples **after cropping** on a grid "determined by `@sar`" —
   i.e. **not** square-pixel-corrected; the pixel shape is carried by `@sar`. So a 720x576 SAR 64:45
   Representation is `@width=720 @height=576 @sar="64:45"` (not 1024x576).
3. Absent `@sar` with `@width`/`@height` present means "as if 1:1" for the size grid, but `@sar`
   *itself* "if not present on any level, the value is unknown". An unknown SAR therefore must not be
   advertised as `1:1`; omitting the attribute is correct.
4. The three attributes inherit: present on the AdaptationSet they apply to every Representation
   unless overridden ("present on any level").

Together with ETSI TS 103 285 §4.4 (`isobmff-tkhd.md` §3: `@sar` "should be present", `@par` "should be
present" on the video Adaptation Set, one of them "shall" be present when the picture aspect ratio is
not 16:9), these are the full normative statements the DASH packagers rely on.

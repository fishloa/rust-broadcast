# Fixture Provenance

Every committed test fixture in `fixtures/` must have a documented
source and a clear legal basis for redistribution. Non-redistributable
material lives in `private/` git submodule, never here.

## Caption/subtitle fixtures (`fixtures/captions/`, `fixtures/subtitles/`)

documented here honestly rather than left to imply otherwise; the fourth is a
real, permissively-licensed subtitle file.
